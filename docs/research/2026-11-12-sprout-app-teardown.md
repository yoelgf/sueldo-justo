# Sprout ("Tinder for jobs") teardown

- What: iOS app (formerly "Prep AI", developer Magnifex AI Inc.). User uploads a CV once, swipes on jobs; on right swipe the AI tailors CV and cover letter and an AI agent applies on the employer's site (optional review first). App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6740011494
- Traction (third-party, unverified): ~$150K/month, 750K+ users; App Store ~4.6 with ~40-50K ratings. Source: https://www.stork.ai/blog/this-ai-job-app-makes-150kmo-applying-for-you
- Model: weekly subscription; core features paywalled.
- Complaints: bugs, crashes, applications not processing.
- Market: US-focused; no sign of Spain coverage.
- Competitors: Sorce (swipe-to-apply), Scout (two-sided swipe, outcome-priced), 200+ iOS alternatives.
- Why it works: gamified swipe, desperate high-volume applicants, swipe demo suits short-form video.

## Fit for a Spanish version
- Pros: clone-for-Spain play, mobile, swipe mechanic, real pain, ceiling in Yoel's target range.
- Cons: Spanish jobs live mostly on InfoJobs/LinkedIn/Indeed, so auto-apply means logging in as the user (ToS risk); growth relied on short videos; AI cost per application (probably cents with a small model; to verify).
- Straight clone scored ~18-19/30; the no-scraping variant (user submits) 21/30.
