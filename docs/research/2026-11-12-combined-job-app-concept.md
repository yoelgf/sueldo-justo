# Combined concept: swipe job app + convenio checker

Yoel's direction (2026-11-12): likes the Spanish Sprout idea, wants it combined with the "¿Me pagan bien?" convenio checker; scope should not be only Comunidad Valenciana - maybe Cataluña or the whole of Spain.

## One app across the job lifecycle
1. Search: swipe job cards, each with a salary badge from the convenio knowledge base (above/below convenio minimum, estimated net per month).
2. Apply: AI-tailored CV + carta per offer; user sends with one tap that opens the offer (no scraping, no auto-apply).
3. Sign: upload the contract; check convenio category, hours, trial period, salary.
4. Get paid: monthly nómina check (pluses, pay rises applied).
5. Leave: finiquito/indemnización check, vida laboral reader, paro estimate.

Why combined: retention after hiring (Sprout-style apps lose users when they get a job), revenue at each stage (weekly sub while searching; monthly plan or one-off checks after; lawyer referral fees), and the convenio knowledge base is a moat both halves share.

## Scope: national, not one region (Claude's notes)
- Many salary tables are provincial (e.g. hostelería, comercio usually by province), so "whole Spain" is a data problem, not a code problem.
- Suggested path: start with state-wide (estatal) convenios, which apply everywhere with one table (to verify per sector: e.g. consultoría, contact center, ETT, grandes almacenes), then add the provincial convenios for the biggest job markets (Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Málaga, Sevilla...).
- Cataluña: large market, but convenios published in the DOGC and partly in Catalan; the AI handles the language, the knowledge base has to cite the source bulletin.
- National scope also helps the job feed and SEO (salary-badge pages per sector/province).
